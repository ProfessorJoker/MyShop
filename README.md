# AALAZ

### Shop smarter. Live better.

AALAZ is a modern, Pakistan-focused online marketplace designed to make shopping simple, affordable, fast, and accessible for everyone.

AALAZ brings customers, sellers, brands, and local businesses together in one convenient shopping platform.

---

## 🇵🇰 About AALAZ

AALAZ is being developed as a next-generation e-commerce marketplace for Pakistan.

The platform is designed around the shopping habits and needs of Pakistani customers, with support for local sellers, Pakistani Rupee (PKR), local delivery, cash on delivery, digital payments, product reviews, seller stores, discounts, vouchers, and personalized shopping.

Our goal is simple:

> **Shop smarter. Live better.**

## 📦 Orders & Delivery

AALAZ will support a complete order workflow:

1. Customer places an order
2. Seller receives the order
3. Seller confirms the order
4. Product is prepared
5. Order is handed to delivery
6. Customer receives tracking updates
7. Order is delivered
8. Customer can review the product

The platform will be designed with Pakistani delivery systems and logistics in mind.
